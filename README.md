# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across time, regions, states, product categories, sub-categories, and products. It also tracks key business KPIs and Year-over-Year performance to identify major changes and performance drivers.

## Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide a clear overview of sales performance and help answer key business questions such as:

* How are sales changing over time?
* Which regions generate the highest sales?
* Which product categories contribute most to total sales?
* Which sub-categories and products perform best?
* How are Sales, Cost, Profit, and Quantity changing compared with the previous year?
* Which areas may require further investigation?

## Key KPIs

For the displayed February period, the dashboard reports:

| KPI            |     Value | YoY Change |

| Total Cost     |  6,498.92 |     -5.99% |

The calculated profit margin for the displayed period is approximately **38.43%**.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Sales Trend

Sales increased from **3,880.06 in 2014** to **4,312.96 in 2015**, representing approximately **11.16% growth**.

However, sales dropped significantly in 2016 to **1,258.20**, a decrease of approximately **70.83%** compared with 2015.

Sales declined again in 2017 to **1,103.73**, representing an additional **12.28% decrease** compared with 2016.

This indicates a significant decline in sales performance after 2015.

### 2. Category Performance

Technology is the strongest-performing category, generating **5,168.16**, which represents approximately **49.0% of total sales**.

Furniture generated **3,867.79**, representing approximately **36.6%** of total sales.

Office Supplies generated **1,518.99**, representing approximately **14.4%** of total sales.

Technology is therefore the main contributor to overall sales.

### 3. Regional Performance

The highest-performing regions are:

* South: **3,440.70**
* Central: **3,361.71**
* East: **2,598.77**
* West: **1,153.76**

South and Central together account for approximately **64.4% of total sales** for the displayed period.

The West region has the lowest sales contribution and may require further investigation.

### 4. Sub-Category Performance

The highest-performing sub-categories shown in the dashboard are:

* Accessories: **4,112.43**
* Chairs: **3,699.84**
* Phones: **1,055.73**
* Storage: **728.82**
* Paper: **597.07**

Accessories and Chairs are the strongest sub-category contributors in the displayed analysis.

### 5. Product Performance

The dashboard highlights the Top 10 products based on sales.

The highest-performing product shown is:

**Global Push Button Manager's Chair, Indigo — 3,117.57**

The second-highest product is:

**Enermax Aurora Lite Keyboard — 2,813.40**

These products contribute significantly to the sales generated by the displayed period.

### 6. Year-over-Year Performance

The dashboard shows a mixed YoY performance:

* Sales decreased by **12.28%**
* Cost decreased by **5.99%**
* Profit decreased by **19.51%**
* Quantity increased by **137.50%**

The increase in quantity alongside the decline in sales and profit is an important business signal. It suggests that the increase in the number of units sold did not translate into higher revenue or profit and should be investigated further through product-level pricing, discounts, and profitability analysis.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Technology is the largest sales category, while the South and Central regions are the strongest regional contributors.

The most important performance concern is the decline in sales and profit despite a significant increase in quantity. The sharp decline in annual sales after 2015 also indicates a need for deeper investigation into product performance, pricing, discounts, and regional trends.
